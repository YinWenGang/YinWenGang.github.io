Privacy Policy

This application (rclone) is a personal desktop utility configured solely for individual use by the account owner.

Data Access and Usage

  • The application accesses Google Drive solely to read, write, and synchronize files directly between the user's local device and their own Google Drive account.
  • OAuth tokens and configuration are stored locally on the user's personal computer.
  • No personal data or Google Drive content is collected, transmitted to, or shared with any third-party servers.
